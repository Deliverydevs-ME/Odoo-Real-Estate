From Broker Registration to the Last Installment

Off-plan developers sell most of their inventory through hundreds of external agencies and brokers, collect payment over years rather than at the point of sale, and pay commission only once the buyer has actually paid. That usually means separate spreadsheets for the broker registry, the payment schedules, the collections follow-up and the commission sheet, none of which agree with each other.

This module puts the whole cycle on standard Odoo CRM, Sales and Accounting. Agencies and brokers are registered once with their Trade License, ORN and BRN documents; leads route themselves to the right Relationship Manager; a sales order on a unit generates the buyer's payment schedule; installment invoices and reminders go out on their own; and commissions become payable exactly when the agreed collection rule is met.

The result is one system for inventory, receivables and the broker network, with the dashboard numbers coming straight from posted invoices and payments.

Built for How Off-Plan Developers Actually Sell

How a Sale Flows Through the Module

From registering an agency to paying its commission.

STEP 01

Register Agencies and Brokers Once

Each agency has a single profile, with its brokers linked to it. The module blocks duplicates on name (legal suffixes such as LLC or FZE are ignored), Trade License, ORN and BRN, and shows a merge warning when a similar name already exists.

Registration documents (Trade License, ORN Certificate, BRN Card, MOA, IBAN letter, KYC file, Emirates ID / Passport and agency agreement) are stored with their files and expiry dates, and are reachable from a Documents smart button on every agency and broker.

STEP 02

Renewals Are Tracked for You

A daily job reminds the Relationship Manager before each document expires (60, 30 and 7 days by default, configurable per document type). When a required document expires, the agency's registration is suspended or cancelled automatically, according to your company setting, and reactivated when the renewed document is uploaded.

The registration status follows through everywhere: leads show the agency's registration state, and commissions for an inactive agency are blocked until it is reactivated.
STEP 03

Leads Route to the Right Relationship Manager

Each CRM lead is categorised as End Customer, Agency, Broker or Investor. The agency is identified from the lead's contact or from its email domain (public domains such as Gmail or Outlook are ignored), and the lead is assigned to that agency's Relationship Manager and territory.

Broker and investor leads go to their own dedicated sales teams, and a sales admin can mark each lead as reviewed.

STEP 04

Sell a Unit on a Payment Plan

Projects hold their units with building, floor, type (studio to villa, plot, retail or office), area, view and price. Selecting a unit on a sales order line fills in the product, price and unit details. Choosing a payment plan generates the buyer's schedule: any down payment percentage, any number of installments and interval, plus handover and post-handover milestones.

The schedule can be edited for each buyer and rebalanced to the order total. Confirming the order books the unit and invoices anything already due; the unit becomes Sold as soon as the down payment is received. Cancelling the order releases the unit again.

STEP 05

Collect, Then Pay Commission

A daily job creates each installment invoice a set number of days before it falls due, optionally confirms it, and sends the buyer a reminder before the due date and again at regular intervals while it is overdue. Commission is calculated from the agency's agreement and plan, with rates that can differ by project and unit type, and vendor bills are created straight from the commission records.

WHEN COMMISSION BECOMES PAYABLE
On sale confirmation, in proportion to what the buyer has paid, or in full once the buyer has paid the agreed threshold.

On confirmation: the unit is booked, the 10% booking invoice is created immediately, and a commission record is created for the agency under the standard plan (2%, payable once 10% has been collected).

When the booking is paid: the unit is marked Sold, the collection threshold is reached, and the agency's commission becomes payable, provided its registration is still active.

Over the following years: each construction installment and the handover payment is invoiced and reminded automatically, and the dashboard's expected cash inflow updates as each payment arrives.

Before You Go Live

The module is built for Odoo 19 Enterprise and depends only on the standard contacts, crm, sale_crm, sale_management, account and mail apps. After installing, set up your territories and Relationship Managers, the broker and investor lead teams, and the invoicing and reminder days under Real Estate → Configuration.

The included payment plans, commission plan and document types are starting templates. Review them against your own sales policy and agency agreements before the first live sale.

AI extraction is optional: digital PDFs such as DED licenses are read from their text layer without AI. For scanned documents, choose a provider and enter its API key under Settings → Contract Migration, and always review extracted records in the migration batch before importing them.
